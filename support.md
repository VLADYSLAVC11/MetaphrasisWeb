## Common questions

### Does Metaphrasis work offline?

Mostly. Translation works offline once the languages it needs are on your
iPhone, and speech recognition works offline for languages your iPhone can
recognise on its own. DeepL and Google need a connection, as does any language
that is not downloaded yet.

### How do I add a language?

Open Settings › Languages in the app. It shows what is already on your device
and what can be downloaded.

### Why do DeepL and Google need my own API key?

They are paid services, billed to your own account with them. Metaphrasis adds
nothing to their price. Add a key in Settings › Privacy & Security, then choose
the order the engines are tried in under Settings › Translation.

### How do I get a more natural voice?

Open Settings › Voices in the app and choose a language. Enhanced and Premium
voices sound far better than the default, and are downloaded in the iPhone's
own Settings app (search for "Voices"). The language's screen in Metaphrasis
explains where.

### How do I hold a conversation across two phones?

Both phones need Metaphrasis and need to be near each other. On one phone,
start a new conversation and choose **Two phones**. On the other, tap **Join a
nearby conversation**. The first phone asks whether to let it join; check that
both screens show the same code, then allow it.

Allow Local Network access when iOS asks: that is how the phones find each
other.

### Metaphrasis can't hear me

Check that it has access to the microphone and to speech recognition, in the
iPhone's Settings app under Metaphrasis.

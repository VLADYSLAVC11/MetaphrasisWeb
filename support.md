## Common questions

### Does Metaphrasis work offline?

Mostly. Translation works offline once the languages it needs are on your
iPhone, and speech recognition works offline for languages your iPhone can
recognise on its own. Camera translation always works offline. Metaphrasis
Cloud, DeepL and Google need a connection, as does any language that is not
downloaded yet.

### How do I add a language?

Open Settings › Languages in the app. It shows what is already on your device
and what can be downloaded.

### How do I translate text from a photo?

Tap the camera button beside the star. Point at the text and take the photo,
or pick one from your library. The text appears as the source, ready to
translate. The yellow brackets show the block of text that will be read.

### How do I hold a conversation across two phones?

Both phones need Metaphrasis and need to be near each other. On one phone,
start a new conversation and choose **Two phones**. On the other, tap **Join a
nearby conversation**. The first phone asks whether to let it join; check that
both screens show the same code, then allow it.

Allow Local Network access when iOS asks: that is how the phones find each
other. Conversations across two phones, and with AirPods, need Basic or
Premium.

### What is a Private conversation?

A conversation started as **Private** keeps everything on the phone: speech is
recognised on the device, and no cloud engine or cloud voice is used. A
language your iPhone cannot recognise on its own is typed instead.

### How do I get a more natural voice?

Open Settings › Voices in the app and choose a language. Enhanced and Premium
voices sound far better than the default, and are downloaded in the iPhone's
own Settings app (search for "Voices"). The language's screen in Metaphrasis
explains where. On Premium, the same screen also offers four Metaphrasis Cloud
voices for each language.

### Why do DeepL and Google need my own API key?

They are paid services, billed to your own account with them. Metaphrasis adds
nothing to their price. Add a key in Settings › Privacy & Security, then choose
the order the engines are tried in under Settings › Translation.

### Metaphrasis can't hear me

Check that it has access to the microphone and to speech recognition, in the
iPhone's Settings app under Metaphrasis.

## Plans and subscriptions

The [Plans]({{ '/plans/' | relative_url }}) page compares Free, Basic and
Premium.

### How do I subscribe, change or cancel a plan?

In the app, open Settings › Usage and Plan. Subscriptions are handled by the
App Store: **Manage Subscription** there opens your App Store subscriptions,
where you can change or cancel a plan. A cancelled plan lasts until the end of
the period you paid for.

### How does the free trial work?

New subscribers can try Basic free for 3 days, once per Apple ID. Cancel at
least 24 hours before the trial ends if you don't want it to become a paid
Basic monthly subscription.

### I bought a plan but the app still shows Free

Open Settings › Usage and Plan and tap **Restore Purchases**, signed in with
the Apple ID you bought it with.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are made by Apple. Request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### My allowance ran out

Settings › Usage and Plan shows how much of each allowance is left. When one
runs out, Metaphrasis uses the engines on your iPhone until the plan renews,
and Metaphrasis Cloud comes back on its own then.

### How do I turn on cloud speech recognition?

On Basic or Premium, turn on **Metaphrasis Cloud** in Settings › Speech
Recognition. Basic uses it in conversations; Premium everywhere in the app.

## Report an ad {#report-an-ad}

The Free plan shows ads from Google. If you see an ad that is offensive,
misleading or inappropriate, email
[{{ m.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ m.contact_email }}) with:

- a screenshot of the ad, if you can take one;
- roughly when you saw it, and where in the app (before a conversation, or
  between translations);
- what the ad was for, or the advertiser's name.

We pass it to Google and block the advertiser where we can.

## Your data

Metaphrasis has no account, so most of what it knows stays on your iPhone and
is removed when you delete the app. To ask about or delete what Metaphrasis
Cloud keeps about your subscription, email
[{{ m.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ m.contact_email }}) with the **Order ID** from
your App Store receipt email. The [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})
explains what is kept and for how long.

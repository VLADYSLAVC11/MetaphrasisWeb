recognises speech, translates and reads aloud in the cloud.

<ul class="plan-notes">
  <li>New users get a one-week free trial of Basic monthly. It becomes the $9.99 month unless you cancel it first. The trial includes 15 minutes of cloud speech.</li>
  <li>Basic's cloud speech is for conversations only. Premium's works everywhere in the app.</li>
  <li>Prices are US prices. The App Store shows the price in your country.</li>
  <li>When an allowance runs out, the engines on your iPhone take over until the plan renews.</li>
</ul>

Plans are bought, renewed and cancelled in the App Store, and managed in the
app under Settings › Usage and Plan.
